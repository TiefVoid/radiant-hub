The Deep Ethereal

Past the foggy barrier that separates the Material from the other Planes, known as the Border Ethereal, you may find yourself in the Depths. A disorienting realm of churning mist, rolling fog, and distant glow, only the most brave dare to explore it. Beware the cyclones that rove across the Deep Ethereal, or other extraplanar travelers with nefarious intentions.

There is, however, one safe haven in an otherwise uninhabitable realm: The Radiant Citadel.

The Radiant Citadel

In the furthest reaches of the Deep Ethereal, coiled around an indestructible gemstone, sit the fossilized remains of a massive unknown entity. Out of this fossil a city has been carved, with pathways and dwellings, for the purpose of commercial and cultural trade.
